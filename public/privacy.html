JeevDristi Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Who we are

JeevDristi is provided by Healthsense Solution PVT LTD. For privacy questions or account deletion requests, contact rndoffice@iitbhilai.ac.in.

This policy covers the authentication-only version of the JeevDristi mobile app and its local connection to the POCT box. It does not describe older versions that used additional cloud services.

Our privacy approach

We do not collect patient records, test results, diagnostic images or reports on our servers. These remain on your phone or connected POCT box. We do not use Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, advertising SDKs, cloud clinical storage or automatic usage tracking. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

The app still processes information needed to sign in. Firebase Authentication is our only Firebase service. Therefore, authentication information is sent to Google; “no data collected” does not describe the authentication process.

Authentication information

When you create an account or sign in, Google Firebase Authentication processes your email address, account identifier, credentials, verification status and authentication activity. Your display name is stored in your authentication profile when you provide it. Passwords are submitted to Firebase Authentication for verification; do not send passwords to our support team.

If you choose Google Sign-In, Google provides the identity information needed to authenticate you, which may include your name, email address and profile picture. Guest sign-in creates an anonymous Firebase authentication identifier. Authentication sessions and preferences may be cached on your device to support offline operation.

Google processes technical information, including IP addresses, to operate and protect authentication. Firebase states that Authentication retains logged IP addresses for a few weeks and other authentication information until account deletion. See Firebase privacy information and Google's Privacy Policy. Authentication may be processed outside your country under Google's applicable terms.

Information kept locally

The app processes operator-entered patient details, screening information, images, results, reports, locally registered box identities and connection settings to provide its diagnostic workflows. These are kept on the phone or POCT box rather than uploaded to Firebase or our servers. Optional location access may add GPS coordinates to local field-screening records and reports. Calculation inputs are processed locally.

Selected patient-identifying fields have local encryption protection; this is not a claim that every database field or exported file is encrypted. Protect access to your phone, box and exported files.

Permissions and device access

  • Camera and selected images: scan QR/barcodes and capture or import images for local analysis.
  • Bluetooth and nearby devices: discover and communicate with the POCT box.
  • Location: optional local report geotagging and Wi-Fi discovery where required by the operating system.
  • Biometrics or device credentials: authorize local access and approved exports. The app does not receive your fingerprint or face template.
  • Notifications: local notifications, without Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • Internet: authentication, public app/firmware update discovery and downloads, and links you choose to open. Public update hosts receive normal network request information such as your IP address; patient records are not included.
  • APK installation on Android: allow an operator-requested app update, subject to operating-system approval.

You can manage permissions in your device settings. Denying a permission may make its related feature unavailable. Google Play services used for barcode scanning may download scanner components and process service information under Google's terms; barcode content is processed locally.

Exports, support and third parties

Clinical records leave the local workflow only when you choose an export or sharing action. Phone Export Link transfers selected records directly over USB to an approved browser session; it does not upload them to a website server. The browser holds the session data in memory, and downloaded files remain on the receiving computer. You are responsible for the recipients and storage of exported records.

If you send a support email, we receive the email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. The app prepares diagnostic details such as app version, device/OS information and signed-in email for you to review in your email client before sending. Please exclude patient information, credentials and clinical images. We use support correspondence to answer your request and retain it while needed for that purpose or applicable legal obligations.

Google authentication, public download hosting, external websites and your email provider have their own privacy terms. We may disclose information we hold when required by applicable law.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain in this installation. The app's local deletion controls hide records from normal views; database entries or artifact files may remain until you clear the app's storage. Uninstalling or clearing the app does not remove records retained on the POCT box, previously exported copies, operating-system backups or your Firebase account. Manage those copies separately. Signing out does not delete your account or local records.

To request deletion of your JeevDristi account, use the account deletion request option in the app's Profile section, or email rndoffice@iitbhilai.ac.in with the subject “JeevDristi account deletion request” from your registered email address. Include your registered email address, but no password or patient information. We may verify that you control the account before deleting its Firebase Authentication record. This deletes the app account; it does not delete your Google account. We will explain any information that must be retained for a legal obligation. Google's limited security-log retention is described above.

For patient records held by a clinic or operator, contact that clinic or operator, who controls the phone, box and exported copies. We cannot remotely erase clinical records because we do not receive them.

Intended users and changes

JeevDristi is intended for authorized adult operators, not for children to create accounts. Operators may handle patient records only with appropriate authorization, including for children.

We will update this policy when our practices change and revise the date above. Privacy or deletion enquiries can be sent to rndoffice@iitbhilai.ac.in.
